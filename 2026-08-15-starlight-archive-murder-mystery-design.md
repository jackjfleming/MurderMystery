# Design Spec: The Starlight Archive Sweet 16 Murder Mystery Party Kit

**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Target Audience:** 16-Year-Old Guests (Sweet 16 Party)  
**Guest Count:** 16 Guests (All 16 Character Roles Filled)  
**Theme & Aesthetic:** Vintage Library Semi-Formal (Dark Academia, Midnight Blue, Brass, Aged Parchment, Velvet)  
**Venue:** Home / Library Setting  
**Host Roles:**
- **John (The Curator):** Played by Son (Dramatic arrival collapse at the gallery archway 2 minutes into the party, then retires to bed).
- **Detective Jack:** Played by Dad (In-world lead investigator, moderates Q&A, presents evidence drops, and delivers the grand finale reveal).
- **Head Archivist Whitney:** Played by Wife (In-world game director, manages check-in, distributes custom notebooks, evidence packets, round dialogue/witness slips, UV flashlights, and ballot box).

## 1. Executive Assessment & Narrative Structure

### Core Narrative Alignment
- **Setting:** *The Starlight Archive* (A restricted historical repository)
- **Secret Society:** *The Starlight Circle*
- **Victim:** John — The Curator
- **The Murderer (Character):** Raelin — Keeper of Keys
  - **Fictional Action:** The character Raelin intentionally and knowingly applied the hazardous archival fumigation compound (Jar E) to the brass lock of Display Case RB-1147, using her CATCH Certification (Certification for Archival Toxicology & Chemical Handling) knowledge that the organomercury compound is lethally toxic through skin contact, to prevent the Curator's midnight presentation from exposing her family's fraudulent history.
- **The Player Experience (Raelin):** 
  - **Hidden-Murderer Mechanic:** The real person playing Raelin is **deliberately not told** that her character committed the crime. Raelin receives standard player materials containing her character's public role, background, personal secret, and investigative objectives. She investigates the mystery genuinely alongside the other 15 guests. Only during the Detective's Final Reveal does she discover that the character she portrayed was secretly the murderer all along.
- **The Motive / Family Secret (Gap A Progression):**
  - *Throughout the Game:* Clues establish that John uncovered a scandalous 1926 family secret involving Raelin's great-grandfather Arthur that would strip her family of their hereditary Keeper of Keys title. The exact nature of this scandal remains an unresolved mystery throughout Rounds 1–3.
  - *The Final Reveal:* Detective Jack dramatically unveils the **1926 Menagerie Invoice**, revealing that Arthur purchased 37 live ferrets on a bulk discount to frame an innocent colleague and steal the title!

### Spatial Staging: The Main Hall & Gallery Archway (Gap C)

- **The Staging Area:** Guests gather in the **Main Reception Hall**. Directly adjoining the hall is an open architectural archway looking directly into the **Rare Books Gallery**, where Display Case RB-1147 is prominently staged.
- **The Action:** John staggers into the Main Hall, reaches the gallery archway threshold in full view of all 16 guests, touches the lock, and collapses.
- **The Evidence Logs:** The electronic logs show the main gallery doors were secured at `23:42`, explaining why only John was logged crossing the threshold at `23:50` and why guests truthfully state they never entered deep into the restricted gallery room.

---

### Evidence Item Progression: The Maintenance Kit (Gaps D & E)

- **Round 1 (Introduction):** The **Chemical Preservation Kit** (Jars A–E) is introduced on the Evidence Table as an archival restoration exhibit. Jar E is labeled as *"Jar E: Archival Fumigation & Anti-Pest Sealant"* with a hazardous materials warning. Players observe it, but cannot draw conclusions yet.
- **Round 2 (Ambiguity):** Players investigate the tea cup, blade, and scan book spines with UV flashlights to find the word **D-E-R-M-A-L**.
- **Round 3 (Significance):** The Toxicology Report reveals that the lethal compound is an organomercury-based archival fumigation sealant that is naturally UV-fluorescent. Players scan the Chemical Kit with UV flashlights, and **Jar E glows neon green**, proving the hazardous preservation compound was applied to the lock handle! The Conservation Log reveals two people (Raelin and Charlotte) hold CATCH certification and Chemical Kit access—removing any single-suspect chemical silver bullet and forcing players to cross-reference master key access and verified timeline alibis to identify the true culprit!

---

### Intentional Grammar Clue in the Curator's Note (Gap G)

In Evidence Item 10, the Curator's handwritten note reads:
> *"Tonight, I will prove who tampered with the brass **LOCK** before midnight. If you were hoping to cover your tracks, you **WAS** sorely mistaken. Everything that was **TAMPERED** with in the gallery archives will be brought to light."*

- **Intentional Design Clue:** The grammatical anomaly *"you WAS sorely mistaken"* was deliberately planted by John. It serves as an intentional signal to the Circle that the letter contains a hidden message. When the laser-cut decryption mask is overlaid, it isolates: **“ITEM RB-1147 LOCK WAS TAMPERED”**.

```

- Conservation Log reveals both Raelin and Charlotte are CATCH-certified with Kit access. Charlotte is exonerated by her verified front desk alibi and lack of Master Key #000.
- Guests use the xTool P3 Laser-Cut Overlay Mask over John's note to read: "ITEM RB-1147 LOCK WAS TAMPERED".

⏱️ 2:00 - 2:30 | 🏆 Reveal, Awards & Sweet 16 Birthday Cake

- Presentation of Sweet 16 Award Certificates + Birthday Cake!
```

## 3. Physical Maker Props (xTool P3 Specifications)

### 🪵 xTool P3 Laser-Cut Overlay Cipher Mask
- **Dimensions:** 5" × 7" cut from 3mm Plywood or Dark Acrylic.
- **Vector Layers:** `#FF0000` = cut lines (4 word cutouts), `#000000` = decorative border engraving.
- **Corner Registration:** Built-in corner alignment notches ensure immediate, foolproof alignment over the printed parchment note.

### 🧪 Physical Chemical Kit
- **Wooden Rack:** Holds 5 vintage glass apothecary jars.
- **Inscriptions:**
  - Jar A: *Parchment Sizing Reagent*
  - Jar B: *Antique Brass Polish*
  - Jar C: *Bookbinder Solvent #3*
  - Jar D: *Brass Lock Solvent & Fingerprint Remover*
  - **Jar E:** *Archival Fumigation & Anti-Pest Sealant* — ⚠ HAZARDOUS (Naturally UV-fluorescent; **GLOWS NEON GREEN** under 395nm UV blacklight). Fill with UV-reactive highlighter liquid!
