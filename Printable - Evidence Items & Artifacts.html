WELCOME TO THE STARLIGHT ARCHIVES

Tonight, you have gathered for an exclusive after-hours meeting of the Starlight Circle—a secret society of writers, scholars, archivists, and insiders who hold special clearance inside the Starlight Archives. At 12:00 AM, the archives' most restricted vault was scheduled to open to reveal Catalog Item RB-1147: The Starlight Ledger.

THE LEAD-UP TO TONIGHT: Over the past week, the Curator (John) audited the archives' restricted movement logs and security archives. He discovered serious irregularities: unlogged entries into restricted areas, unauthorized access, defaced artifacts, and an embarrassing historical family scandal. John privately confronted multiple members, warning that his audit was complete and that every secret would be exposed during tonight’s 12:00 AM presentation.

THE INCIDENT: Moments after guests gathered in the Main Reception Hall, the Curator entered clutching a coat sleeve tear and holding his arm in pain. Seconds later, in full view of everyone through the gallery archway, he reached out toward Display Case RB-1147, touched its brass lock handle, and dramatically collapsed to the floor—dead.
