OFFICIAL SOCIETY CHARACTER ROSTER

Advance Guest Guide & Character Assignments for the Restricted Vault Gathering
WELCOME TO THE STARLIGHT ARCHIVES: You are cordially invited to an exclusive after-hours gathering of the Starlight Circle. Below are your assigned character roles and personalities for the evening. Review your persona and get to know your fellow circle members before arriving! Full confidential character packets will be distributed at the event.
RAELIN – Keeper of Keys
The trusted keeper of the archive's master keys, visitor sign-in logs, and restricted vault doors. Detail-oriented, watchful, and fiercely protective of who gets access.
CLAIRE – Order Keeper
The archive’s strict rule enforcer who keeps order, manages schedules, and tracks conduct files for the Starlight Circle. Super organized, by-the-book, and notices every broken rule.
SARAH – Nocturnal Photographer
The archive’s official after-hours photographer who captures rare books, historic architecture, and late-night secret meetings. Creative, observant, and never seen without a camera.
BROOKLYN – Underground Publisher
An independent writer and publisher of underground mini-magazines and secret newsletters. Rebellious, witty, and always hunting down juicy rumors and club drama.
CHARLOTTE – Rare Book Specialist
A book-loving scholar who tracks down, inspects, and catalogs priceless ancient books for the archive's private collections. Sharp-eyed, studious, and obsessed with old mysteries.
CHRISTINA – Silver-Tongued Debater
The Circle’s smoothest speaker and master debater. Charismatic, quick on her feet, and capable of talking her way into—or out of—any situation.
VICTORIA – The Annotator
A patient researcher fascinated by handwritten notes and doodles scribbled in the margins of centuries-old books. Thoughtful, observant, and drawn to the personal secrets left behind by past readers.
ELLIE – Puzzle Tinker
A clever tinkerer who specializes in antique brass locks, hidden puzzle boxes, and secret coded locks. Curious, hands-on, and loves cracking complex mechanisms.
GIANNA – Amateur Sleuth
A curious and perceptive archive regular with a knack for chasing down rumors and solving real-world mysteries. Quick-witted, persistent, and always taking notes.
HOPE – Literary Prodigy
A celebrated young author and poet famous in the Circle for her gripping historical stories and dramatic poetry. Creative, ambitious, and deeply inspired by classic literature.
GEORGIA – Archivist of Secrets
The friendly desk archivist who organizes the library card catalogs, records, and front-door check-ins. Welcoming, organized, and knows where every single item is hidden.
KAITLIN – Silent Strategist
A quiet, sharp mastermind who works behind the scenes to influence club rules, group votes, and secret decisions. Calm, strategic, and never misses what’s happening in the room.
MADDIE – Conservatory Reader
A devoted lover of classic books, quiet reading traditions, and protecting peaceful study nooks in the library. Passionate, book-smart, and protective of the archives' quiet spaces.
RUTHANNE – Library Darling
A cheerful archive volunteer and club favorite celebrated for organizing charity book drives and welcoming guests. Outgoing, friendly, and popular with everyone in the Circle.
LUCY – Aesthetic Curator
The creative stylist who designs the library’s visual vibe, antique room displays, and moody lighting for special events. Artistic, trendy, and focused on making everything look aesthetic.
VALE – Unfortunate Witness
A helpful archive assistant who helps set up rooms, move historic documents, and manage library supplies. Hardworking, observant, and quietly notices everything happening around her.
