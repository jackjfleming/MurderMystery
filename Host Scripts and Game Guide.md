# **Starlight Archives Murder Mystery — Host Scripts & Game Guide**

This document contains all updated host scripts, stage directions, and instructions for running the Starlight Archives Murder Mystery party. It incorporates the instant murder opening at the gallery archway, the 3-track deduction system, the Physical Chemical Kit with Jar E UV fluorescent testing, the CATCH certification mechanic, the xTool P3 Laser-Cut Overlay Cipher, the Parent Whispering Prompts, and Detective Jack's grand finale reveal of the **1926 Menagerie Invoice (37 Ferrets)**!

---

## **🎬 Opening Sequence & Party Arrival (Immediate Staged Murder)**

**Host Setup:** Gather all 16 guests in the **Main Reception Hall** as soon as everyone has checked in with Archivist Whitney and received their custom detective notebooks and character dossiers. The Chemical Preservation Kit (Jars A–E) is visible on the Evidence Table.  
**Stage Directions:** John (The Curator) enters the hall clutching a visible tear in his coat sleeve / holding his arm in pain. Right as Detective Jack finishes the opening welcome below, John gasps, steps to the adjoining **Rare Books Gallery Archway**, reaches out toward Display Case RB-1147, grips the brass lock handle, and dramatically collapses to the floor! After a few moments, John quietly slips away to go to bed!

**HOST SCRIPT — OPENING & ARRIVAL (Detective Jack):**  
"Welcome to the Starlight Circle at the Starlight Archives!  
Tonight, you are writers, scholars, and insiders gathered for an exclusive after-hours ritual built around access, reputation, and hidden knowledge. At 12:00 AM, the archives' most restricted vault was scheduled to open to reveal Catalog Item RB-1147: The Starlight Ledger.  
Over the past week, many of you received private letters, warnings, or direct confrontations from the Curator. He claimed to have uncovered unlogged breaches, altered records, and an embarrassing family secret, warning that everything would come to light during tonight's presentation.  
Look! Here comes the Curator now—"  
*(John staggers to the gallery archway, touches the RB-1147 lock, and DRAMATICALLY COLLAPSES!)*  
"Lock the front doors! Nobody leaves this room!  
Inside the Rare Books Gallery... **THE CURATOR WAS MURDERED!**  
The killer is standing in this room right now. We will examine the evidence in three stages to uncover who killed the Curator and why."

## **🏆 Final Spoilers & Reveal Script (Read After Voting)**

**Host Setup:** Collect all Voting Ballots. Dramatic pause between sections. Detective Jack holds up the **1926 Menagerie Invoice** prop.

**HOST SCRIPT — FINAL REVEAL (Detective Jack):**  

**PART 1: The Incident**  
"Tonight's mystery began at the Starlight Circle inside the Starlight Archives. Writers, scholars, and insiders gathered for an exclusive after-hours meeting built around access, reputation, and dangerous knowledge.  
Everyone arrived expecting the 12:00 AM presentation—the moment when the archives' most restricted vault would open. But moments after stepping through the doors, the Curator collapsed dead at the threshold of the Rare Books Gallery."

**PART 2: The Red Herrings**  
"At first, suspicion pointed in every direction.  
Did Victoria deliberately stab him with her sharp fountain pen during their furious corridor confrontation? Victoria certainly struck him in a panic! But forensics proved the arm puncture was filled with regular fountain pen ink and was 100% non-toxic.  
Did Hope poison his desk tea with bitter almond extract? Hope certainly tried! But John gave up caffeinated tea weeks ago on his doctor's orders—the cup remained completely untouched.  
The real murder weapon was waiting for him at the gallery display lock."

**PART 3: The Truth Unraveled**  
"The toxicology report confirmed the lethal dose was an organomercury-based archival fumigation compound—a legitimate preservation chemical used to protect rare manuscripts from bookworm and mold—coated directly onto the brass lock of Catalog Item RB-1147.  
When we shined our UV blacklights on the Chemical Preservation Kit, **Jar E—the Archival Fumigation & Anti-Pest Sealant**—glowed bright neon green!  
Now, TWO people in this circle had CATCH chemical certification and Chemical Kit access: Charlotte, the Rare Book Specialist, and Raelin, the Keeper of Keys. Both knew that Jar E was lethally toxic through skin contact. Charlotte even borrowed Master Key #000 earlier this afternoon, raising suspicions that she might have used it or made a wax copy!  
But Ellie verified that the lock was turned by the original antique Master Key #000 (recovered right in the courtyard bushes), not a copy. And Georgia and Claire proved Charlotte was sitting with them at the front intake desk the entire time!  
Only ONE CATCH-certified person possessed Master Key #000 tonight AND had an unaccounted 15-minute prep gap to slip through the courtyard window, coat the brass lock with Jar E, and toss the key into the bushes.  
And why was this person so terrified of John's midnight presentation?"

**PART 4: The Grand Motive Reveal & Verdict**  
*(Detective Jack dramatically unfolds the 1926 Menagerie Invoice)*  
"Because John discovered this: An official 1926 receipt from Barnaby's Menagerie & Exotic Animals!  
It proves that Arthur—her great-grandfather—negotiated a special volume bulk discount on **THIRTY-SEVEN LIVE FERRETS**, which he released into the archives during the 1926 Annual Gala to frame Julian Vance and steal the hereditary Keeper of Keys title!  
The entire family legacy was a ninety-nine-year ferret fraud!  
The person who wiped the lock to protect her family's 37-ferret secret is...  
**THE KEEPER OF KEYS! *(Raelin)!***"
