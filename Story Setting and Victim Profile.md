## **📜 Story & Setting – Read This First**

**WELCOME TO THE STARLIGHT ARCHIVES.**  
Tonight, you have gathered for an exclusive after-hours meeting of the *Starlight Circle*—a secret society of writers, scholars, archivists, and insiders who hold special clearance inside the Starlight Archives. At 12:00 AM, the archives' most restricted vault was scheduled to open to reveal Catalog Item RB-1147: The Starlight Ledger.  

**THE LEAD-UP TO TONIGHT:**  
Over the past week, the Curator (John) began auditing the archives' restricted movement logs and security archives. He discovered serious irregularities: unlogged entries into restricted areas, unauthorized access, defaced artifacts, and an embarrassing historical family scandal. In the days leading up to tonight's event, the Curator privately confronted multiple members of this circle, warning that his audit was complete and that every secret would be publicly exposed during tonight’s 12:00 AM presentation.  

**THE INCIDENT:**  
Moments after guests gathered in the Main Reception Hall, the Curator entered clutching a coat sleeve tear and holding his arm in pain. Seconds later, in full view of everyone through the gallery archway, he reached out toward Display Case RB-1147, touched its brass lock handle, and dramatically collapsed to the floor—dead.  

**THE STAKES & RULES:**  
The killer acted just in time to stop the Curator’s presentation. The archive doors are locked, and no one can leave. Examine the evidence, cross-reference timeline gaps, interrogate your fellow suspects, and uncover who committed murder to protect their secret!

## **👤 The Victim Profile: John — The Curator**

**Name:** John  
**Title / Role:** The Curator of the Starlight Archives  
**Status:** Deceased *(Collapsed at the Rare Books Gallery Archway)*  

**BACKGROUND & PERSONALITY:**  
John served as the chief overseer of the Starlight Archives' restricted vault and historical collections. Meticulous, stern, and unflinchingly devoted to rules, John viewed the archives as a sacred trust. He was highly respected—and deeply feared—for his ability to uncover hidden details that others overlooked.  

**WHAT WAS HE DOING BEFORE THE MURDER?**
> * **The Audit:** Spent the past 7 days reviewing electronic access logs, keycard records, and physical ledger stamps.  
> * **The Confrontations:** Sent private letters, cease-and-desist orders, and formal warnings to several members of the Starlight Circle regarding plagiarism, stolen artifacts, forged credentials, and unlogged entry breaches.  
> * **The Final Movement:** Arrived at the archives shortly before guests were admitted. He engaged in a minor hallway altercation right before stepping through the hall, clutched an injured arm, touched the brass lock of Item RB-1147 at the gallery archway, and collapsed instantly.

**KNOWN EVIDENCE FOUND ON / NEAR THE VICTIM:**
> * **On His Person:** A minor coat sleeve tear and small surface scratch on his left forearm *(Preliminary findings: sustained during an argument earlier today)*.  
> * **On His Desk:** A ceramic tea cup with saucer smelling of tea and concentrated bitter almond infusion.  
> * **At His Desk Safe:** A confidential handwritten note left for the Starlight Circle, cryptically noting that Item RB-1147 was tampered with before midnight.
